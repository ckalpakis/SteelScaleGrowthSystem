# Steel Scale Systems — Reusable Loom Demo Script

**Purpose:** One evergreen walkthrough you record once and send to *every* prospect.
Nothing in it names a specific person or company, so it never goes stale.

**Target length:** 3 minutes (range 2:30–3:30).
**Format:** Loom, camera bubble on (bottom corner) + screen share.
**What to have open in tabs before you hit record:**
1. `steelscalesystems.com` (your landing page)
2. `steelscalesystems.com/site/demo` (the finished demo site)
3. `steelscalesystems.com/login` → the client dashboard (logged into the demo client)

> Legend: **[SHOW]** = what's on screen · **[SAY]** = word-for-word narration.
> Say it in your own voice — this is a script, not a teleprompter. Keep energy up and talk like a person.

---

## 0:00–0:20 — Hook (camera full screen or landing page)

**[SHOW]** Your face, or the Steel Scale Systems landing page.

**[SAY]**
"Hey — quick 3-minute walkthrough of what we build for local businesses.
In short: we give you a professional website *and* the system to turn the visitors
it brings in into booked jobs — all in one place. Let me just show you, it's easier
to see than explain."

---

## 0:20–1:15 — The website (the demo site)

**[SHOW]** Switch to the demo site tab. Slowly scroll the homepage top to bottom.

**[SAY]**
"This is a live example site. First thing to notice — it looks like a real, established
company, not a cheap template. It's fast, it works perfectly on a phone, and every
section is built to do one job: get the visitor to reach out."

**[SHOW]** Point cursor at the hero headline, the star rating, and the quote form.

**[SAY]**
"Right at the top there's a clear headline, your reviews for trust, and a quote form
that's always one tap away. As I scroll — your services, real reviews from customers,
photos of past work, the areas you serve, and answers to the questions people always ask."

**[SHOW]** Click into one service page, then hit back. Click "Areas" / a town, then back.

**[SAY]**
"Every service and every town you work in gets its own page — that's what helps you
actually show up on Google when someone nearby searches for what you do. And the whole
thing is branded to *you* — your logo, your colors, your photos, on your own domain."

---

## 1:15–2:30 — The system behind it (the dashboard)

**[SHOW]** Switch to the dashboard tab (logged in). Land on the main dashboard/overview.

**[SAY]**
"Now here's the part most 'website companies' don't give you. When someone fills out
that form, they don't just send you an email and disappear — they land right here, in
your own dashboard."

**[SHOW]** Point to the KPI cards (Total Leads, Won Revenue, Pipeline Value, Conversion Rate)
and the "Leads Over Time" graph. Toggle the graph between **Leads** and **Revenue**.

**[SAY]**
"You get a simple, at-a-glance view of your business — how many leads you're getting,
how many you've won, your revenue, and how it's trending over time. No spreadsheets,
no guessing."

**[SHOW]** Click into Leads. Show the pipeline columns. Open one lead.

**[SAY]**
"Every lead moves through a simple pipeline — new, contacted, estimate scheduled, won.
You always know exactly where everyone stands. Open a lead and you've got their contact
info, what they need, their budget, and a spot for notes so nothing falls through the cracks."

**[SHOW]** Point to the "Request a Review" / review buttons on the lead.

**[SAY]**
"And once a job's done — one click sends that happy customer a review request, by text
or email. That's how you stack up 5-star reviews, which brings in the *next* batch of
leads. It's a loop."

**[SHOW]** Briefly show the "Needs Response" banner and/or the Export CSV button.

**[SAY]**
"You even get a heads-up when a new lead needs a reply — because speed is everything —
and you can export everything any time. It's your data."

## 2:30–3:00 — How you get started (make it feel easy)

**[SHOW]** Back to the landing page, or your face on camera.

**[SAY]**
"Here's how simple it is on your end: you don't touch any of the tech. We build the
whole thing for you — website, branding, all of it — get your approval, and launch it
on your domain. From there, leads just start showing up in your dashboard. If you can
check email, you can run this."

---

## 3:00–3:20 — Close / call to action

**[SHOW]** Your face on camera (best for the ask), landing page behind you.

**[SAY]**
"If you want, I'll put together a quick example for *your* business so you can see exactly
what it'd look like — no cost, no commitment. Just reply to this and we'll set up a
15-minute call. Thanks for watching — talk soon."

## Delivery tips (don't read these on camera)

- **Keep moving.** Scroll and click while you talk; dead air on a static screen feels long.
- **Smile on the hook and the close** — those are the two moments the camera bubble matters most.
- **Don't over-explain features.** Sell the outcome: more leads, more booked jobs, more reviews.
- **One take is fine.** A slightly imperfect, human video converts better than an over-polished one.
- **Trim the ends** in Loom after recording so it starts and ends crisp.
- **Set a custom thumbnail** (the demo homepage or your smiling face) — it lifts the open rate.

## How to reuse it
Because nothing here names a person or company, the same recording works for every
prospect. Personalize the *message you send with it* instead — e.g. "Made this quick
video showing how we'd get [their company] more booked jobs — 3 min, worth a look."
Re-record only when you change pricing or add a major new feature.

## Optional 90-second cut (for cold outreach / ads)
Use just these beats: **Hook (0:00)** → **website scroll (0:20)** → **dashboard + leads
graph (1:15)** → **review-request loop (one line)** → **Close/CTA**. Skip the service/area
pages and the "how you get started" section.
