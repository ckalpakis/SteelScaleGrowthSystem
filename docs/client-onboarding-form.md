# Free Homepage Mockup — Client Onboarding Form (Google Forms build sheet)

Copy each question below into Google Forms. Every field maps to something in the
Steel Scale dashboard, so a completed form is everything you need to build the mockup.

**Legend:** `[Type]` = the Google Forms question type · **Required** = make it required.
Text in _italics_ under a question is the **help/description** to paste into that field.

> **Google Forms setup tips**
> - Turn on **"Collect email addresses"** (Settings → Responses) so you always have a reply-to.
> - Group questions into the **sections** below (use "Add section"). It makes a long form feel short.
> - **File upload questions require the respondent to be signed into a Google account.** If your
>   clients may not be, replace the upload questions with a "paste a link / we'll email you" note
>   (there's a ready alternative under each upload question).
> - Set the **confirmation message** (bottom of this doc) so they know what happens next.

## Section 1 — Your business

1. **Business name** — `[Short answer]` · **Required**
   _Exactly how you want it to appear on your website._

2. **Your name** — `[Short answer]` · **Required**
   _Who we'll be working with._

3. **Best phone number** — `[Short answer]` · **Required**
   _This becomes your website's "Call Now" button._

4. **Best email** — `[Short answer]` · **Required**
   _Where new leads and updates get sent._

5. **Business address** — `[Short answer]`
   _Street, city, state, ZIP. Used for your map and local SEO. (Leave blank if you work from home / no storefront.)_

6. **Primary city / area you serve** — `[Short answer]` · **Required**
   _e.g. "Pittsburgh, PA." This is your main SEO location._

7. **Business hours** — `[Short answer]`
   _e.g. "Mon–Sat: 7am–7pm."_

8. **How many years have you been in business?** — `[Short answer]`
   _We use this for trust ("Family-owned since 2003", "20+ years", etc.)._

## Section 2 — What you do

9. **List your services** — `[Paragraph]` · **Required**
   _One per line, most important first. e.g._
   _Roof Replacement_
   _Roof Repair_
   _Gutters_
   _Siding_

10. **Which service makes you the most money / do you most want to promote?** — `[Short answer]`
    _We'll feature this first._

11. **Cities / towns / neighborhoods you serve** — `[Paragraph]`
    _One per line. Each becomes its own page to help you rank on Google locally. e.g._
    _Pittsburgh_
    _Mt. Lebanon_
    _Cranberry Township_
    _Wexford_

---

## Section 3 — Your brand & look

12. **Do you have a logo?** — `[Multiple choice]` · **Required**
    - Yes — I'll upload it below
    - No — I need one / use my business name for now

13. **Upload your logo** — `[File upload]` (allow images/PDF, up to 2 files)
    _Highest quality version you have (PNG with transparent background is ideal)._
    _**No-Google-account alternative:** replace with a Short answer — "Paste a link to your logo (Google Drive, Dropbox, website), or reply to our email with it attached."_

14. **Main brand color** — `[Short answer]`
    _A hex code (e.g. #b91c1c) if you know it, or just describe it ("deep red", "navy blue"). We'll match your brand._

15. **Do you have a current website?** — `[Short answer]`
    _Paste the link, or write "None." If you have one, tell us what you do and don't like about it._

16. **Do you already own a domain (web address)?** — `[Short answer]`
    _e.g. "yourbusiness.com," or "No, need one."_

17. **A short tagline or slogan (if you have one)** — `[Short answer]`
    _e.g. "Pittsburgh's Trusted Roofers." Optional — we can write one for you._

---

## Section 4 — Your story & why people choose you

18. **In a sentence or two, what makes your business different or better?** — `[Paragraph]` · **Required**
    _This becomes your headline and pitch. Talk like you would to a customer._

19. **Top 3 reasons customers choose you** — `[Paragraph]`
    _One per line. e.g._
    _Family-owned and operated_
    _Free, no-pressure estimates_
    _Licensed, insured, and fully warrantied_

20. **Licenses, certifications, or trust badges** — `[Paragraph]`
    _One per line. e.g. "Licensed & Insured (PA123456)," "BBB Accredited," "GAF Certified." These build instant trust._

21. **A short "About" — tell us your story** — `[Paragraph]`
    _A few sentences on who you are, how you started, and why you care. We'll polish it. Optional but recommended._

---

## Section 5 — Reviews & social proof

22. **Google star rating** — `[Short answer]`
    _e.g. 4.9. Leave blank if you're just getting started._

23. **Number of reviews** — `[Short answer]`
    _e.g. 196._

24. **Link to your Google reviews** — `[Short answer]`
    _Paste your Google Business Profile / reviews link._

25. **Paste 2–3 of your favorite customer reviews** — `[Paragraph]`
    _Include the customer's first name and town if you can. e.g._
    _"Best roofers in Pittsburgh — fast, fair, spotless cleanup." — Karen M., Mt. Lebanon_

26. **Social media links** — `[Paragraph]`
    _Facebook, Instagram, Google Business — one per line. Optional._

---

## Section 6 — Photos

27. **Upload photos of your work** — `[File upload]` (allow images, up to 10 files)
    _Real before/after and finished-job photos beat stock every time. Even phone photos are great._
    _**No-Google-account alternative:** replace with a Short answer — "Paste a link to a folder of your photos (Google Drive, Dropbox, Google Photos), or reply to our email with them attached."_

28. **Do you have a favorite hero/banner photo?** — `[Short answer]`
    _Tell us which one (by filename) or describe the vibe you want up top. Optional._

---

## Section 7 — Wrap up

29. **Who are your main competitors, or any websites whose look you like?** — `[Paragraph]`
    _Paste links. Helps us nail the style you want._

30. **Anything else we should know?** — `[Paragraph]`
    _Special offers to feature, things to avoid, questions — anything goes._

31. **Best way and time to reach you to review the mockup** — `[Short answer]`
    _e.g. "Call after 4pm," "Text is best."_

| Brand color | Brand color |

| Top 3 reasons | Value props |
| Certifications | Trust badges / badge logos |

**Tip:** keep this as a repeatable intake. One completed form = one client you can build in the
dashboard in ~20 minutes without chasing them for details.
